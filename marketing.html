FAQ

Common Questions

What is hotel revenue management?
Hotel revenue management is the practice of dynamically pricing rooms based on demand, seasonality, and competitor rates to maximize total revenue. We combine AI-driven tools with expert strategists to balance occupancy and ADR for partner hotels.
How does an AI-powered channel manager help my hotel?
An AI channel manager automatically syncs your room inventory and rates across OTAs (Booking.com, Expedia, MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Agoda) and your direct site, prevents overbookings, and recommends optimal rates in real time — saving hours of manual work and lifting RevPAR.
How do I increase hotel bookings with digital marketing?
A combination of SEO (so travellers searching "hotels in Goa" find you), Google Ads (capturing high-intent searches), social media (Instagram + Reels for visual destinations), and reputation management (4.5+ ratings drive 2-3x more clicks). We build all four pillars together.
Do you work with small or independent hotels?
Yes. We work with boutique hotels, homestays, resorts and small chains across India. Our Silver plan is designed for owners just starting their digital journey.
Are there any contracts or lock-in periods?
No long-term contracts. We work on rolling monthly engagements — if we're not delivering value, you walk. 87% of our clients renew quarter-on-quarter.
