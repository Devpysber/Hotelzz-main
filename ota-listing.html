FAQ

Common questions

How long does it take to get my hotel listed?
Most OTA registrations go live within 7–14 days. Booking.com and Agoda often verify within 3–5 days. We handle every follow-up call, document request and rejection appeal.
What documents do I need to provide?
Owner ID proof, hotel registration certificate (or GST), property photos (we use Google + your uploads), and a bank account for OTA payouts. We send you a single checklist after onboarding.
Do I pay OTA commissions separately?
Yes. Hotelzz only handles one-time registration. OTAs charge 12–20% commission on confirmed bookings, paid directly to them. Our fee is one-time, no recurring.
What if an OTA rejects my listing?
We re-submit with corrections at no extra cost. If still rejected after 2 attempts (usually due to property type / location restrictions), we refund that OTA's prorated share.
Can I add more OTAs later?
Yes. Add additional OTAs anytime at the same per-OTA rate. Existing customers get 10% loyalty discount on add-on listings.
Do you also manage OTAs after they go live?
Yes — that's a separate OTA Management service under our Marketing plans. Rate optimisation, content updates, review responses, channel sync.
